

# Aura Pulse90 — Future Roadmap

The roadmap for Aura Pulse90 was intentionally designed around phased product maturity rather than feature accumulation.

The goal is to strengthen behavioral consistency, improve personalization quality, and scale retention systems gradually over time.

---

# Phase 1 — Stability & Foundations

Focus:
- Improve reliability
- Strengthen analytics visibility
- Reduce technical risk

Planned improvements:

- PostHog analytics integration
- Improved recovery scoring logic
- Better onboarding flows
- Error boundary implementation
- Dashboard performance optimization
- Firebase cleanup and optimization
- Expanded workout library

---

# Phase 2 — Personalization & Intelligence

Focus:
- Deeper AI personalization
- Better adaptive recommendations
- Smarter wellness insights

Planned improvements:

- Advanced AI coaching memory
- Adaptive workout recommendations
- Dynamic recovery-based programming
- Smarter nutrition recommendations
- Personalized wellness insights
- Voice-based AI interaction
- Enhanced behavioral tracking

---

# Phase 3 — Growth & Ecosystem

Focus:
- Long-term retention
- Ecosystem expansion
- Community reinforcement

Planned improvements:

- Wearable integrations
- Social accountability systems
- Community challenges
- Progress sharing features
- Native mobile app packaging
- Advanced gamification systems
- Group-based wellness journeys

---

# Long-Term Product Vision

Aura Pulse90 is intended to evolve beyond a traditional fitness tracker into a behavioral wellness operating system.

The long-term vision focuses on:

- adaptive behavioral coaching
- emotionally intelligent wellness systems
- long-term consistency reinforcement
- AI-assisted accountability
- recovery-aware personalization
- sustainable lifestyle transformation

---

# Product Prioritization Philosophy

Roadmap decisions are guided by:

- behavioral impact over feature quantity
- retention over short-term engagement spikes
- simplicity over unnecessary complexity
- sustainable usage over intensity optimization
- product clarity over feature accumulation

Features are intentionally added gradually to preserve usability, reduce cognitive overload, and maintain behavioral focus.
